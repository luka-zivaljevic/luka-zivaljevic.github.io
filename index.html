About

I'm an Industrial Engineering student at Purdue University with a hands-on background in software, applied AI, and CAD design. Most of what I know came from building things end to end: designing the part in Fusion 360, printing it, wiring it, writing the firmware, and then figuring out why it doesn't work yet.

Before Purdue I played collegiate water polo at Long Beach City College: 16 hours of training a week, 30+ games a season. That's also where I learned to teach: coaching 10-and-unders at Pegasus Aquatics and teaching 3D printing, laser cutting, and robotics to 1st–8th graders at Forge MakerSpace.

Right now I'm starting Zival LLC, building educational boxes that teach kids the vocabulary of machine learning through physical puzzles, with no screens required. I'm looking for an internship in industrial engineering or operations.
